1. Project Vision 
MHIndiaTrips 
 is  
not 
 a simple travel agency website. 
The goal is to build one of the most premium India-focused tourism platforms for international  
travelers 
. 
This website should inspire visitors to travel to India before they even contact the company. 
The design must feel like a combination of: 
National Geographic 
Airbnb 
Aman Resorts 
Four Seasons

Apple 
The website must emotionally sell India through beautiful visuals, storytelling, destination discovery, and rich travel information.

The website will focus exclusively on  
Domestic Tourism in India 
 for international visitors. 
The primary objective is: 
Generate travel inquiries 
Build trust 
Showcase India professionally 
Rank on Google through content marketing 
Become a travel knowledge platform instead of just a tour booking website

Instead, every package and destination should encourage visitors to request a customized itinerary.
